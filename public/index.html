    The basics ?

    These are the core facts every streaming service (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.) needs about your release.

    Discovery & genre ?

    Genre and mood tags are how playlists, algorithms, and curators find you. Pick what fans would search for, not just what sounds impressive.

    Rights & identifiers ?

    UPC/EAN identifies the whole release; your distributor usually assigns one for free. ISRC identifies each individual track and is how streams get tracked back to you — also usually assigned by your distributor, but worth recording here once you have it. The © line covers the sound recording; the ℗ line covers the composition/publishing.

    Cover art

    Square image, at least 3000×3000px recommended for DSP review.

    Tracklist & credits ?

    List every track with writer/producer/performer credits — this is what you'll need for royalty splits and PRO registration later, so get it right from day one.

    Bio & pitch ?

    A short bio and a one-line pitch make it far easier for blogs, playlist curators, and radio to cover you. Keep the pitch to 1-2 sentences.
